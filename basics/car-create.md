# 🚗 Building Your Car Card

The **Car Creation Wizard** is a guided 5-step flow that sets up your vehicle's digital identity, baseline specs, and build goals. Access it from the **+** quick-action menu → **Add Car**.

---

## Step 1: Choose Your Creation Method

Pick the pathway that fits your situation:

| Method | Badge | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| 🔢 **VIN Number** | ✅ Recommended | Any production car with a VIN label |
| 📷 **AI Photo Scan** | Medium | When your VIN isn't handy |
| ✍️ **Manual Entry** | ⚠️ Least Recommended | Custom builds, exotics, JDM imports, rare or vintage vehicles |

---

## Step 2: Photo & Exhaust Audio (All Methods)

Upload or capture your vehicle's hero photo:
* **Gallery** or **Camera** — your photo becomes the face of your 3D Car Card.
* A live preview of your card renders in real time as you select an image.

**Optional — Record Your Exhaust Note:**
* 🎙️ **Record Engine Live**: Start recording with the in-app mic. A real-time animated waveform visualizes your audio as you record. Playback, re-record, or delete before saving.
* 📁 **Upload Audio File**: Import an existing `.mp3`, `.wav`, or `.m4a` clip from your device.

Your exhaust note is embedded into your Car Card and plays when viewers tap the audio icon.

---

## Step 3: Vehicle Details

### VIN Mode
Enter your 17-character VIN. The NHTSA VPIC decoder instantly auto-populates:
* Make, Model, Model Year, Trim level
* Drivetrain (RWD / FWD / AWD / 4WD)
* Engine displacement, cylinder count, fuel type
* Body class and GVWR

A **"Verify Instantly with VIN"** shortcut also appears during database entry to upgrade to full verification.

### Database / Standard Mode (for non-VIN entry)
Searchable dropdown modals guide you through:
**Make → Model → Year → Trim → Drivetrain**

Optionally add a custom **Car Name** (e.g. *"Project Midnight"*, up to 24 characters) — displayed across your card and profile.

### Manual Mode
Type Make, Model, Year, Trim, and select Powertrain (Combustion / EV) and Drivetrain chips. Hit **"Verify with AI"** to cross-reference your entered specs against live automotive databases (Edmunds, MotorTrend, Car and Driver) for plausibility validation.

---

## Step 4: Performance Specifications

* **Database / VIN builds**: OEM factory specs auto-fill (marked with their source: `OEM_DB`, `TEMPLATE`, or `USER_ENTERED`). These are immutable for verified builds.
* **Manual builds**: Enter Engine/Motor configuration, Horsepower, Torque (lb-ft), Curb Weight (lbs), and 0–60 mph time. A **live estimated OVR Score** calculates in real time as you type.

---

## Step 5: Build Intent & Goals

Before your card is minted, Carbase captures your intent so the AI Advisor can give targeted recommendations:

| Field | Options |
|---|---|
| **Primary Goal** | Faster acceleration / more power · Better handling · Better braking · More aesthetic / visual presence · Balanced |
| **Secondary Goal** | Any of the remaining goals (optional) |
| **Use Case** | Daily driver · Weekend fun · Track / autocross · Show / photos |
| **Budget Range** | $ · $$ · $$$ · $$$$ · Prefer not to say |
| **Risk Preference** | Conservative · Moderate · Aggressive |

Tap **Create Car Card** — your card is minted and you're taken to the interactive **Car Card Tutorial** to learn gyroscope tilt, foil interactions, and sharing.
