# 🚀 Getting Started

When you launch **Carbase**, setting up your verified vehicle identity is simple. We provide multiple precision pathways to create your vehicle profile and generate your dynamic 3D Car Card.

---

## 1. 🔢 NHTSA VIN Decoder *(Recommended)*

The fastest and most authoritative method. Enter or scan your 17-character Vehicle Identification Number:
* Carbase directly interfaces with the official **NHTSA VPIC Automotive Database** to pull verified factory specifications: Make, Model, Model Year, Trim, Drivetrain (AWD/RWD/FWD/4WD), Engine Displacement, Cylinders, Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR), and Factory HP/Torque baselines.
* Supports both modern cars and offline motorcycle WMI matrix decoding (Yamaha, Ducati, Harley-Davidson, Honda, BMW, Kawasaki, etc.).

**Where to find your VIN:**
* On the lower driver-side corner of your windshield.
* Inside the driver-side door jamb certification sticker.
* On your vehicle title, registration certificate, or insurance card.

---

## 2. 📷 AI Vision Photo Scanner

Take or upload a photo of your vehicle:
* **Multimodal Vision Engine:** Instantly recognizes the make, model, estimated year range, trim tier, body style, and powertrain configuration from vehicle exterior photos.
* **VIN OCR Scanning:** Point your camera at a VIN barcode, door-jamb sticker, or registration document to auto-read the 17-character sequence.
* **Rejection Safeguards:** Automatically verifies that the uploaded image contains an authentic motor vehicle.

---

## 3. ✍️ Smart Manual Entry with Web Verification

For custom builds, classic vintage models, track kit cars, or JDM imports where standard VIN decoding might be incomplete:
* Input Make, Model, Year, Trim, Powertrain, and Performance specs manually.
* **Automated Web Plausibility Engine:** Carbase cross-references entered specs against real-world automotive benchmark databases (Edmunds, MotorTrend, Car and Driver) with automated schema validation to verify that claimed horsepower, torque, curb weight, and 0–60 mph acceleration times are mechanically plausible.

---

## What Happens Next?

1. **Interactive 3D Car Card Generated:** Your vehicle card is minted with interactive tilt physics, dynamic lighting shaders, and customizable skins.
2. **Baseline OVR Score Calculated:** Your initial Overall Rating (0–100) is established across 5 foundational pillars.
3. **Garage Slot Assigned:** Your build is showcased in your virtual 3D showroom, ready for modification logging, exhaust note recording, and community sharing.
