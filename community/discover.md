# 🔭 Discover & Explore Builds

The **Discover** tab in Carbase is the global showroom and exploration engine for community builds, live leaderboards, and real-world automotive events.

---

## 🔍 Global Search & Category Filters

Easily explore builds across the platform using multi-faceted filters:
* **Enthusiast Categories:** Euro, JDM, Muscle, EV, Track, Off-Road, Classic, Supercars.
* **Powertrain Sorting:** Combustion vs. All-Electric EV setups.
* **Verification Status:** Filter specifically for 100% VIN-verified builds with attached dyno/receipt proof.
* **Garage Power Index (GPI) & OVR Score:** Sort by highest rated builds globally, nationally, or within a 500-mile local radius.

---

## 📅 Curated Real-World Automotive Events

Carbase actively aggregates upcoming automotive events, including:
* **National Collector Auctions:** Barrett-Jackson, Mecum Auctions, RM Sotheby's.
* **Auto Shows & Expos:** SEMA Show, Tokyo Auto Salon, Goodwood Festival of Speed, local Cars & Coffee meets.
* **Track Days & Time Attack Competitions:** High-performance driving events and autocross schedules.

---

## 📂 Collection Binder & Saved Builds

When exploring community builds:
* Tap **Save to Binder** to bookmark inspiration builds directly to your personal collection (`app/collection.tsx`).
* Inspect detailed build stages, part numbers, dyno power curves, and exhaust audio clips.
* Directly message the vehicle owner to discuss part fitment or project advice.
