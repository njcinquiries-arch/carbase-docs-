# 👥 Crews & Car Clubs

**Crews** in Carbase are car clubs where builders connect, chat, share builds, and compete on crew leaderboards.

---

## 🏷️ Creating a Crew

Tap the **+** quick-action menu and select **Start Crew** to open the crew creation modal. Required fields:

* **Crew Name**: Up to 32 characters. Filtered for inappropriate content before publishing.
* **Description**: Up to 200 characters describing your crew's focus and identity.
* **Category**: Select one of the 10 preset categories (see below).
* **Privacy**: Toggle **Private Crew** to require join approval from founders/admins before anyone can enter.
* **Logo**: Choose from preset category logos or upload a custom square photo from your camera roll.
* **Community Rules Agreement**: Founders must agree to enforce the 4 platform community rules before creating.

> **Free tier:** Create 1 crew. **Carbase PRO ($4.99/mo):** Create unlimited crews.

## 💬 Inside a Crew

### Live Chat
Real-time crew messaging with photo attachments and interactive embedded Car Card widgets. Messages sync across all members instantly.

### 1-on-1 Direct Messages
Private conversations with any crew member directly from the Groups hub.

### Build Transformation Feed
Post before/after build updates. Members can react with **Helpful** upvotes or **Save** posts to their collection binder.

### Daily Crew Challenges
Rotating cooperative challenges requiring multiple members. Completing them earns XP for all participants and raises the crew's overall platform standing.

### Optional Live GPS Meetup Map
Members can optionally share location during crew meets at three granular privacy tiers:
* **Live**: Exact real-time GPS pin visible to crew members.
* **Approximate**: City/neighborhood-level blur only.
* **Private**: Location fully hidden (default).

---

## 🏆 Crew Leaderboard

Each crew maintains an internal leaderboard ranking members by their vehicle's OVR Score. Rankings update live as members verify new modifications and earn score boosts.
