# 🎨 Customization & 3D Styling

The **Customization & 3D Styling** tools in Carbase allow you to personalize your 3D Car Card, making your vehicle's digital identity unique and visually striking.

---

## 🃏 3D Card Materials & Holographic Skins

Carbase features **22 distinct material skins** rendered using real-time Skia 2D shaders and Three.js 3D physics:

| Rarity Tier | Material Skins & Shaders |

| ⚪ **Common / Base** | Clean Factory, Matte Noir, Brushed Aluminum, Classic Slate |
| 🟢 **Uncommon** | Frozen Glaze, Blueprint CAD, Aventurine Green |
| 🔵 **Rare** | Midnight Carbon, Matte Alcantara, Heritage Racing Stripe, Sakura Bloom |

## ✨ Active Underglows & Foil Accents

* **Interactive Gyroscope Tilt:** Tilting your physical device shifts the specular highlights and light reflections across the card surface in real time.
* **Edge Glow Engine:** Responsive vector glow radiating from behind the card edges with customizable RGB hues and pulse frequencies.
* **Custom Sound Clips:** Record your actual exhaust tone, turbo spool, or two-step pops using in-app audio recording and link it to your card for tap-to-listen playback.
