# 🤖 Resident Automotive Advisor

The **Resident Automotive Advisor** inside Carbase is an interactive intelligence assistant engineered specifically for car enthusiasts, builders, and tuners.

Equipped with real-time web search capabilities and deep awareness of your active garage, the Advisor acts as your personal virtual master mechanic.

---

## 🛠️ Integrated Capabilities & Function Calling

The Advisor has direct access to specialized tools to provide context-aware responses:

* **Garage Awareness (`getUserGarage`):** Automatically reads your active vehicle, verified modifications, baseline horsepower, torque, and drivetrain specs to calculate compatibility without you having to re-explain your setup.
* **Community Intelligence (`searchCommunityBuilds`):** Queries thousands of verified builds across the Carbase platform to see what parts other owners of your platform are running.
* **Smart App Routing (`getAppNavigation`):** Directs you straight to builder screens, card packs, or leaderboards when you're ready to execute on a build plan.
* **Live Automotive Search:** Grounded with real-time web search to find current part listings, compatibility bulletins, and aftermarket pricing.

---

## 💡 Core Use Cases

### 1. 🏎️ Build Planning & Staging
* *"What supporting mods do I need before going Stage 2 on my Golf R?"*
* *"Recommend a track-focused coilover and sway bar setup for an FK8 Civic Type R."*
* *"Calculate power gains from high-flow cats, downpipe, and custom tune."*

### 2. 🔍 Fitment & Compatibility Validation
* Checks wheel offsets, bolt patterns, brake clearance, and tire stretch specifications.
* Identifies known factory weak points (e.g., charge pipes, oil pumps, rod bearings) before adding boost.

### 3. 📸 Multimodal Diagnostic Vision
* Upload a photo of a warning dash light, tire wear pattern, engine bay oil seepage, or suspension component.
* The Advisor analyzes the visual cues and outlines potential causes and recommended diagnostic steps.
