# 🏁 Welcome to Carbase

Welcome to **Carbase** — the premier platform for verified vehicle identity, 3D interactive car cards, community crews, and automotive progression.

Whether you're building a dedicated track weapon, an authentic show build, or daily driving something special, Carbase gives your vehicle a dynamic digital identity that evolves with every modification, dyno run, and build stage.

---

### Key Capabilities in Carbase:

* 🔍 **Verified Vehicle Creation:** Build your car profile via NHTSA VIN decoding, AI photo scanning, or manual entry with automated web verification.
* 🃏 **Interactive 3D Car Cards:** Showcase your build with real-time physics, interactive tilt, dynamic holographic foils, 22+ custom skins, and custom exhaust audio playback.
* ⭐ **Dynamic OVR Score & GPI:** Track your vehicle's 5-pillar Overall Rating (0–100) and your Garage Power Index as you log verified parts and synergistic modifications.
* 🤖 **Resident Automotive Advisor:** Get grounded modification suggestions, part fitment guidance, and community build references powered by integrated automotive intelligence.
* 🎨 **Designer Studio:** Generate photorealistic dark-studio concept renders of your vehicle with custom aesthetic and aerodynamic modifications.
* 🎁 **The Vault & Card Packs:** Unlock holographic card skins, carbon textures, visual materials, and custom themes through tiered card packs (Silver, Gold, Ruby, Diamond).
* 📲 **Car Codes (Windshield QR Decals):** Order high-grade 3"x3" kiss-cut vinyl windshield stickers linking anyone directly to your live vehicle showcase card via tap or scan.
* 👥 **Crews & Community Hub:** Found or join specialized car clubs (JDM, Euro, Muscle, EV, Track, Local), chat in real time, share build transformations, and view nearby car meets.
* 🏆 **Rankings & Achievement Badges:** Climb Global, National, and Local leaderboards while unlocking 37 unique progression badges across your automotive journey.
