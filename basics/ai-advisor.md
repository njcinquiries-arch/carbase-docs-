# 🤖 AI Advisor

The AI Advisor isn't a generic chatbot. It's a purpose-built automotive intelligence — six months in the making — trained on real build logs, mod compatibility data, dyno results, suspension geometry, and thousands of real-world car conversations. It knows the difference between a street build and a track setup. It knows what breaks first when you push a stock turbo past its limits. It knows your specific car, your verified mods, and your goals.

Your car. Your build. Your advisor — running on the most advanced AI models available.

***

## What It Does

**Build Planning**

* Suggests modifications tailored to your exact build stage, goals, and budget
* Recommends a logical build sequence so mods don't conflict or require expensive revisits
* Flags compatibility issues before you buy — cross-referencing your specific year, make, trim, and existing verified mods
* Estimates real-world cost ranges for parts, labor, and installation

**Technical Knowledge**

* Explains complex automotive concepts in plain language — gear ratios, suspension geometry, boost curves, fueling requirements, tuning maps, aero theory
* Breaks down dyno graphs, wheel horsepower vs. crank numbers, and what the data actually means for your build
* Covers everything from basic maintenance to forced induction builds, coilover setups, big brake kits, ECU tuning, and beyond
* Advises on towing capacity, payload ratings, track regulations, and emissions compliance

**Diagnostics & Inspection**

* Analyzes photos you upload to identify wear, damage, or abnormal conditions
* Decodes dashboard warning lights — including complex multi-code OBD2 scenarios
* Walks you through step-by-step inspection processes
* Helps you understand what a mechanic's quote actually covers — so you never get overcharged

**Live Part Search**

* Searches the web in real time for verified listings from trusted retailers
* Returns direct product URLs so you can compare and buy instantly
* Filters results by your vehicle's specific fitment — not generic suggestions

**Specs & Research**

* Pulls OEM specs, technical service bulletins (TSBs), known issues, and community insights for your exact vehicle
* Cross-references real mod reviews, dyno results, and build threads from across the enthusiast community
* Keeps up with new part releases, recalls, and platform-specific updates as they happen

***

## How to Use It

Open the **AI Advisor** tab and type your question or goal:

> *"I want to add 50whp to my WRX without touching the engine internals"*
> *"Best suspension setup for track days on a 2021 Supra"*
> *"What does this warning light mean?"* ← attach a photo
> *"Find me an intake for my F80 M3 under $400"*

The Advisor responds with recommendations specific to your verified vehicle and current build stage — not generic advice pulled from a forum.
