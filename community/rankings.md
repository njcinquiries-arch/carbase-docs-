# 🏆 Rankings & Leaderboards

The **Rankings** screen shows where your builds stand against the global Carbase community — across vehicle type, geography, and garage scope.

---

## 🔍 Leaderboard Filters

Switch between 5 filters to narrow the competition pool:

| Filter | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| 📍 **Local** | Builds within a 500-mile radius of your GPS location |
| 🇺🇸 **National** | All public builds registered in your country |
| 🌍 **Global** | Every public build on Carbase worldwide |
| ⛽ **Gas** | Combustion-engine vehicles only |
| ⚡ **Electric** | Electric vehicles only |

---

## 🥇 Leaderboard Tab

A 3D visual podium displays the **Top 3** builds:
* 🥇 Gold podium — #1 ranked build
* 🥈 Silver podium — #2 ranked build
* 🥉 Bronze podium — #3 ranked build

Positions **#4 through #15** are listed below showing: rank position, vehicle thumbnail, car name, owner handle (`@username`), and OVR Score.

---

## 🎖️ Tier Ladder Tab

View every OVR Score rank tier and see exactly where your vehicles land:

| Tier | OVR Score Range | Description |
|---|---|---|
| ⚪ **Rookie** | 0 – 39 | Stock baseline, build just starting |
| 🟢 **Amateur** | 40 – 54 | Light bolt-ons, initial modifications |
| 🔵 **Contender** | 55 – 69 | Balanced street build |
| 🟣 **Advanced** | 70 – 84 | Heavy mods, forced induction or full suspension |
| 🟡 **Elite** | 85 – 94 | High-spec builds with strong multi-domain coherence |
| 🔴 **Champion** | 95 – 100 | Master-tier, competition-grade builds |
| ⭐ **Signature** | ∞ | *"Complete vision, beyond measurement"* — platform prestige |

Each of your cars is placed in its corresponding tier visually on this tab.

---

## 📊 Your Stats Tab

A complete overview of your full garage performance:

### Garage Power Index (GPI)
Your **GPI** is the cumulative score across all vehicles in your garage. It determines your **Garage Tier**:

| Garage Tier | GPI Required |
|---|---|
| 🥉 **Bronze Garage** | 0+ GPI |
| 🥈 **Silver Garage** | 71+ GPI |
| 🥇 **Gold Garage** | 121+ GPI |
| 💠 **Platinum Garage** | 181+ GPI |
| 💎 **Diamond Garage** | 281+ GPI |
| 👑 **Master Garage** | 401+ GPI |

### Garage Vitals
* Total Cars in Garage
* **Build Precision** (Average Coherence % — how synergistically your mods work together)
* Total Modifications Logged
* Verified Parts Count

### Technical Highlights
* Total Combined HP across all vehicles
* Net HP Gain from all installed mods
* Total Combined Torque
* Expert Builds Count (builds above Advanced tier)
