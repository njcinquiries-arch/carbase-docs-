# 🔨 Prestige & Card Ascension System

The **Prestige & Card Ascension System** is Carbase's official card fusion and evolution engine. It solves duplicate card redundancy by enabling collectors to forge duplicate vehicles into their primary builds, permanently elevating their card rarity, performance rating (OVR), and market valuation.

---

## 🎯 Core Principles

1. **Every Car Starts as Common:** All freshly spotted, minted, or acquired cars enter the Collector Dex at **Common** rarity (Tier 0). Supercars retain their high intrinsic performance ratings (e.g. 96 OVR for a Porsche GT3 RS), but their card rarity is grounded at Common until ascended.
2. **Prestige is the Sole Engine of Rarity:** The **only way** to elevate a card from Common to Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, or Mythic is through the **Prestige Forge**.
3. **Earned Prestige:** When a collector flexes an **Epic**, **Legendary**, or **Mythic** card on the Feed or Auction House, the community knows it was not simply spotted — it was forged by sacrificing multiple duplicates.

## 🃏 Graded Card Layout & Rarity Badges

To maximize visual impact and provide clean artwork display, Collector Dex cards follow a graded slab hierarchy:

* **Unobstructed Artwork Face:** The top-left corner of the card face is completely free of text or clutter, showcasing full photographic composition. The official **OVR Score Crest** sits in the top-right corner.
* **Graded Rarity Badge Below Card:** Directly beneath the card slab, each vehicle features an official graded rarity badge pill:
  * **Color-Coded Status Dot:** Glowing dot matching the tier's authentic color.
  * **Title Case Rarity Label:** Clean, elegant typography (`Common`, `Uncommon`, `Rare`, `Epic`, `Legendary`, `Mythic`) instead of loud block capitals.
  * **Prestige Status Tag:** For ascended cars in the Prestige Fleet, an adjacent `P1 • 2X`, `P2 • 3X`, etc. multiplier badge indicates tier standing.

## 🔨 The 4-Step Prestige Forge Flow

When a user acquires a duplicate car of the same make or model, the system flags the vehicle as **Ready to Prestige**:

1. **Chamber Review:** The user enters the Prestige Forge. The **Base Car** and duplicate **Catalyst Car** are displayed side-by-side with verified checkmarks. An ascension bar highlights the pending tier upgrade (e.g. `Common ➔ Uncommon`).
2. **Confirmation Sheet:** The user reviews the permanent fusion terms, noting the consumption of the catalyst duplicate and the required Base Bolts forge fee.
3. **The Forge Strike:** The animated **Forge Hammer** strikes three times against the digital anvil accompanied by synchronized haptic feedback and expanding energy shockwaves.
4. **Ascension Celebration:** The catalyst is consumed, the base car permanently ascends to its new rarity tier with boosted OVR and multiplied Bolt valuation, and an immediate cash-back bonus of Base Bolts is deposited to the collector's wallet.

## 🌐 Ecosystem Integration

* **Public Activity Feed:** Ascensions broadcast to the live Carbase Ledger with customized mini token spec capsules (e.g. `@peak prestiged Porsche • +2 OVR • 2X Value`).
* **Auctions & Marketplace:** Ascended cards command premium valuation and bidding interest due to their boosted multipliers and high OVR scores.
* **Scrap Vault:** Prestiged vehicles yield multiplied Base Bolt returns if ever liquidated in the Scrap Vault.
