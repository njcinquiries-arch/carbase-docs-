# 🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 29, 2026 · Last Updated: August 16, 2026 · Velto Enterprises LLC

**Summary:** Carbase collects vehicle information, account data, modification history, diagnostic logs, and usage metrics to deliver its services. We do not sell your personal information. You can delete your account and data at any time.

---

## 1. Who We Are

Carbase is owned and operated by **Velto Enterprises LLC** ("Velto," "we," "us," or "our"), a technology company building software infrastructure for verified vehicle identities, AI modification advice, and automotive community interactions. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, processing, disclosure, and protection of your information across the Carbase mobile application, web applications, APIs, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

If you have questions about this policy, contact our Data Protection & Privacy Officer at: [privacy@veltoteam.com](mailto:privacy@veltoteam.com)

---

## 2. Comprehensive Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Account Credentials:** Name, email address, username, encrypted password, and social authentication identifiers (e.g., Sign in with Apple ID).
* **Vehicle Data:** Vehicle Identification Number (VIN), license plate numbers, state of registration, make, model, year, trim, factory options, engine specifications, odometer readings, and custom build names.
* **Modification & Build Logs:** Aftermarket part names, manufacturer part numbers, installation dates, dyno charts, performance metrics, sound clips, build stage photos, and costs voluntarily submitted by you.
* **Community & Social Interaction:** Crew memberships, Car Code transactions, public posts, chat messages, user-to-user comments, and trade communications.
* **Payment Information:** Payment processing for Car Codes, VIP passes, and subscriptions is handled exclusively by third-party billing providers (Apple In-App Purchases). We do not collect or store full credit card numbers.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
* **Device & Hardware Data:** Mobile device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (e.g., IDFV), network operator, battery level, and hardware parameters.
* **System Usage & Telemetry:** Feature usage frequency, UI clickstream, session duration, screen navigation paths, latency metrics, and app performance data.
* **Log & Diagnostic Reports:** IP address, crash logs, system call traces, memory state at time of crash, and server request timestamps.
* **Camera & Photo Library Access:** Only accessed upon explicit permission to scan VIN labels, license plates, or upload vehicle photos.

### C. Information From Third-Party Sources
* **Automotive Data Providers:** We query licensed automotive databases using your VIN to retrieve factory build specifications, safety recall notices, and historical market data.
* **AI Infrastructure Partners:** Telemetry metadata regarding AI Advisor interactions to evaluate prompt performance and guardrail accuracy.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process user data strictly for legitimate operational and business purposes, including:
1. Creating, maintaining, and securing your Carbase account and digital vehicle identity.
2. Generating the **OVR Score**, build verification timelines, and algorithmic market value estimates.
3. Powering the **AI Advisor** to generate vehicle-specific modification recommendations, fitment analysis, and maintenance sequencing.
4. Facilitating Crew interactions, Car Code trades, and community features.
5. Detecting, investigating, and preventing fraud, unauthorized access, bot activity, and safety violations.
6. Complying with applicable legal processes, law enforcement requests, and statutory requirements.

## 4. Artificial Intelligence & Data Guardrails

Carbase utilizes advanced artificial intelligence models to deliver personalized recommendations and analytics.

### A. AI Processing Guardrails
* Vehicle specifications and modification queries are processed via encrypted API endpoints with our enterprise AI infrastructure partners.
* **NO GENERAL MODEL TRAINING:** Our enterprise contracts strictly prohibit third-party AI providers from using your personal information or build logs to train public or foundational AI models.

Users assume full responsibility for verifying all information before performing mechanical work.

## 5. Information Sharing & Disclosure

**WE DO NOT SELL YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION.** We share data only under the following strictly controlled circumstances:

* **Public Community Profiles:** Vehicle profiles, build logs, OVR Scores, and photos you set to public will be visible to other platform users.
* **Service Providers:** Trusted vendors performing backend cloud hosting, AI processing, crash logging, and analytics. All vendors are bound by strict confidentiality and non-disclosure obligations.
* **Legal & Law Enforcement Disclosures:** When required by law, subpoena, court order, or governmental regulation, or when necessary to protect the legal rights, safety, or property of Velto Enterprises LLC, our users, or the public.
* **Corporate Transactions:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, re-organization, sale of company assets, or bankruptcy, user data may be transferred to the acquiring entity.

**NO GUARANTEE OF ABSOLUTE SECURITY:** While we strive to protect your data, no security system or internet transmission is completely impenetrable. Velto Enterprises LLC cannot guarantee 100% security against unauthorized hacking, cyber-attacks, or data breaches. You use the App and transmit data at your own risk.

## 7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active.
* **Account Deletion:** You may request account deletion at any time in the App settings. Upon deletion, your account credentials and personal identifiers will be permanently deleted or anonymized within 30 days.
* **Aggregated Data:** Anonymized vehicle telemetry, aggregated modification statistics, and public market trends may be retained indefinitely in non-identifiable form for platform analytics.

## 8. International User Rights (CCPA / GDPR / Global)

### A. California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)
California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, correct inaccuracies, and opt out of any data sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal data. To submit a CCPA request, contact [privacy@veltoteam.com](mailto:privacy@veltoteam.com).

### B. European Economic Area & UK Residents (GDPR)
If you reside in the EEA or UK, our legal bases for processing data are: (i) performance of our contract with you; (ii) explicit consent; (iii) compliance with legal obligations; and (iv) our legitimate interest in providing secure services. You have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port your data, or lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority.
